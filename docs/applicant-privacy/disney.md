# Disney: applicant privacy notes

Source: Disney NGR Privacy Policy (non-guest relationships, covers job applicants), https://sites.disney.com/ngr-privacy-policy/. Read 2026-10-17; the policy can change, so re-check before relying on it.

Public information only. Nothing here is about a particular applicant.

## What the policy says

| Topic | Summary |
|---|---|
| Data collected | Contact details, work authorization, government ID, CV, work history, education, references, optional diversity data. Interviews: image, video and voice from web conferencing, CCTV and security cameras. Third-party background, education, work-performance, credit and criminal checks "only to the extent permitted by law". Device, IP and location data, cookies and pixels. |
| Retention | No fixed period: "for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined". Interview recordings and background checks are not addressed separately. |
| Sharing | Across "The Walt Disney Family of Companies", whose members may use it as their own controller unless local law forbids. Service providers (IT, cloud, banks, background checks). Also "organizations that we believe you are employed by or work with". Corporate transactions. |
| International transfers | Worldwide. Standard Contractual Clauses for EU, UK and Swiss data. |
| Sale / sharing | California section says identifiers, online activity and inferences were "sold" or "shared" in the last 12 months with analytics providers and social networks. Opt-out available, including the Global Privacy Control signal. |
| AI / automated screening | Not mentioned. |
| Rights | Access, correction, deletion, complaint to a regulator. Withdrawing consent or deleting data may end the application. |
| Contact | privacycontact@twdc.com (general), EMEA.dataprotection@disney.com (UK/EU). |

## Application form consents (Disney Careers, Buenos Aires)

| Consent | Required to apply | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Processing of personal information for the application | Yes | Needed to submit. |
| Use of profile for other jobs across the Disney family, including outside your territory | No | Shares the profile with affiliates and enables email about other roles. |
| Company news and talent-community email | No | Marketing-type email. |
| SMS and WhatsApp about the application | No | Messages to the phone number on the profile. |

## Things to weigh before applying

- Interview recording has no stated retention or access limit.
- Affiliates can reuse applicant data for their own purposes.
- No disclosure about AI screening, so ask the recruiter if it matters.
- Tracking data is used for advertising.
